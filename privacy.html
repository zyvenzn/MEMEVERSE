Privacy Policy — MEMEVERSE Terminal

Draft for Release 1.0. This policy must be reviewed and completed by the publisher before Google Play submission.

Last updated: 4 November 2026

What the app does

MEMEVERSE Terminal Release 1.0 is an offline simulated trading application. It generates illustrative market prices and simulated trades using virtual funds only. It does not connect to brokers, exchanges, banks, payment processors or live market-data services.

Information stored on your device

The application stores demo account settings, virtual cash, watchlists, generated chart candles, simulated orders, positions and trade history locally in your browser or Android application storage using IndexedDB. This information is not synchronized to MEMEVERSE servers or other devices in this release.

Information collected or shared

The offline application does not ask for an account, contacts, precise location, camera, microphone or payment information. It does not include advertising or analytics SDKs. The desktop website is hosted by Vercel; the hosting provider may process standard technical request logs under its own policies when the website is accessed online.

Data retention and deletion

Local demo information remains on the device until the user resets the demo account, clears the browser/app storage, or uninstalls the application. Clearing storage or uninstalling may permanently remove local demo data. Backup and restore is not yet included.

Children

MEMEVERSE Terminal is a financial-market simulator and is not designed to provide financial services or investment advice. The publisher must define the intended age rating and comply with applicable platform and local requirements before release.

Security

Because demo data is stored locally, anyone with access to the unlocked device or browser profile may be able to access it. Do not store real financial credentials or sensitive personal information in this application.
